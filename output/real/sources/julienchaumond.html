    Many of you asked for this over the past few weeks...

    Today we are introducing HF-MOUNT 🔥

    → Mount any repo from the Hub (Storage Buckets, but also models, datasets) as a local filesystem

    This is a game changer, as it allows you to attach remote storage that is 100x bigger than your local machine's disk.

    Read-write for Storage Buckets, read-only for models and datasets.

    Here's an example with FineWeb-edu (a 5TB slice of the Web):

    1️⃣> hf-mount start repo datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-edu /tmp/fineweb

    It takes a few seconds to mount, and then:

    2️⃣> du -h -d1 /tmp/fineweb
    4.1T ./data
    1.2T ./sample
    5.3T .

    🤯😮

    Two backends are available:
    NFS (recommended) and FUSE

    This is also perfect for Agentic storage!!     Hugging Face

    We just launched OpenUSD support on the Hugging Face Hub 🎉

    Yesterday, NVIDIA put 2,500+ 3D SimReady assets on the Hub. Forklifts, robot arms, engines, hospital equipment, and more. You can also search the whole library in plain English.

    The viewer is open source. It does USD composition in Rust compiled to WASM and renders with three.js, so you can use it in your own app.

    Datasets with SimReady packages also show a 3D Assets preview on their page, with the number of packages from across the dataset, as on LGElectronics/simready-assets and Vertiv/simready-assets.

    This is the future of 3D and Digital twins! 🔥 h/t Caleb Fahlgren
