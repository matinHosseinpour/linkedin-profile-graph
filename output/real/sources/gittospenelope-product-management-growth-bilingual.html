    Hugging Face Storage Buckets now have an S3-compatible API, and it went GA this morning!

    Any tool that speaks S3 now works against an HF bucket!

    Point it at the Hugging Face endpoint instead of AWS and carry on.

    I spent a day pointing every S3 client I could find at it, and re-ran the whole set against the live release this morning. What worked:

    - aws-cli, boto3, s5cmd, OpenDAL — full read/write
    - Polars, DuckDB, DVC, Daft, WebDataset, MLflow — all fine
    - rclone too (one setting: list_version = 2)

    Eleven clients, all working. And since compatibility comes down to the S3 client library underneath, anything built on those same libraries works the same way.

    More and more people are putting their working data on buckets: they're cheaper than most options, and they're Xet-backed under the hood, which means moving data is very fast. We've found this to be particularly important for AI workloads where you have a lot of similar data (checkpoints, dataset iterations, etc) moving around.

    The S3 API just means you can use the tools you already have.

    Keep your existing scripts, change the endpoint!

    Just out: 🚨 Introducing a revolution for cloud storage

    Hugging Face infra announces their new technology which drastically lower cloud storage costs, accelerated uploads/downloads, and the plan to build the future of storage.

    Yes, it's cheaper than S3.
    And yes, it will enable obvious use cases S3 can't tackle.

    Best part: Storage Buckets on HF are now publicly available !

    It took a lot of effort from the infra and Xet team, and lots of exchanges with the AI community to design this and have it ready for everyone.

    I'm extremely grateful to the Xet/infra team at HF, and the Hub team for putting all this together and offer a new future for storage: more accessible, and more versatile.

    This is the beginning of a new paradigm for storage:

    ✨ Per-TB pricing with built-in CDN and deduplication speedups
    ✨ Raw + processed dataset: stored once, billed once
    ✨ Designed for ML workflows: datasets, checkpoints, model artifacts

    And the craziest parts, which will be available soon (TM):

    💥 Edit data or models files in-place and in one step (no need to download > edit > reupload)
    💥 Merge files and datasets together with *zero-copy*

    All this is enabled by Xet, a deduplication based storage that chunks files to enable fast, cheap storage and zero-copy operations.

    Find more information in the announcement blog post on HF:     https://lnkd.in/eug9W94g
